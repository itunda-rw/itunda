Questions shop owners ask.

Do I need a special device?

No. Any phone or tablet works, including as a customer front screen.

When do I get paid?

Payouts are sent every Monday to your MTN MoMo number.

How much do ads cost?

Each tap costs 100 RWF. You set a daily budget, can pause at any time, and new businesses get 10,000 RWF of free ad credit.

Where do my ads show?

In Benefits and after a payment is done. Ads never appear on balances, in Send or Pay steps, or in Messages. Every ad carries an Ad tag.

Can my staff see my sales?

Cashiers take payments and orders, but can’t see sales, payouts or refunds. Only owners see the full picture.

Does it work for deliveries?

Yes. Eats orders go from you to an itunda Picker. See the Picker prototype.

Can I connect my own system?

Developers can build on itunda. Read the docs at developers.itunda.im.
