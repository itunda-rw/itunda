Pay

Show a code. Or scan one.

Pay in store with itunda Pay. Your code refreshes every minute.

  • Till number for shops without a QR.
  • Pay bills: Electricity, Water, Taxes, School fees and Irembo.
  • Airtime for MTN and Airtel.
  • Transit: Kigali Bus Services, 300 RWF a ride.
  • Split a bill and Receive money.
Card

Your own itunda card, in seconds.

A real debit card for your itunda balance. No paperwork, no waiting.

  • No annual fee, ever.
  • Issued instantly in the app. No branch visit.
  • Pick your finish: Indigo, Midnight or Sand.
  • Your limits: set a daily and a monthly spend limit, and freeze the card in one tap if it is ever lost.
Save

Save a little each day, earn up to 10%.

31-day savings. Your bonus grows at day 7, 14, 21 and 31.

  • Day 7: 2%
  • Day 14: 4%
  • Day 21: 6%
  • Day 31: 10%
  • 26-Week Savings: week 1 is 500 RWF and each week adds 500 RWF more, on a 5% base rate.
  • Interest jar: 4.0% a year, take money out any time. 12-month deposit: interest paid upfront, principal locked.
  • Also in the app: Pockets for school fees and your emergency fund, and Auto-save.
Ikimina

Save with your group.

Join an Ikimina group such as Umurenge Savers. Everyone contributes each round and one member receives the pot.

  • See who has paid this round.
  • Know whose turn is next and when.
  • Contribute your share in one tap.
Invest

Start with a small amount.

Buy shares of Rwandan companies, lend to the Government with Treasury bills, or apply for an IPO. Money for buying comes from your itunda Bank balance.

  • Price alerts when a stock hits your price.
  • Auto-invest a fixed amount every month.

itunda is not a licensed broker and this is not investment advice.

Benefits

Rewards for what you already do.

Points and coupons that come from using itunda, never from chasing them.

  • Invite a friend: share a link, you both get a reward.
  • Card cashback on every itunda card purchase.
  • Shop cashback: 1% back on products you buy in Shop.
  • Daily challenge: a small task for a small reward.
  • Offers from local shops, always marked Ad.
Our promise

An app that does not shout at you.

Ads help local shops reach neighbours, so we keep them calm and honest.

  • No popups. Offers live inside lists you already read.
  • Every ad is labelled with a small Ad tag, and you can hide it.
  • Never near your money. No ads on your balance, when you send or pay, or in messages.
  • Your balance and messages stay private. Ads never use them.
itunda Business

Questions people ask.

Which languages does itunda speak?

English, Kinyarwanda and Français.

Is there a fee for the card?

No annual fee, ever. Your card is issued instantly in the app.

How does the 31-day savings bonus work?

Your bonus grows as you keep saving: 2% at day 7, 4% at day 14, 6% at day 21 and 10% at day 31.

Does itunda show ads?

Offers from local shops are always marked with an Ad tag, and you can hide them. They never appear on your balance, when you send or pay, or in messages.

Is itunda a licensed broker?

No. Investing in itunda is not investment advice. Money for buying comes from your itunda Bank balance.

Can I set limits on my card?

Yes. You set your own daily and monthly spend limits, and you can freeze the card in one tap if it is lost.

How does 26-Week Savings work?

Week 1 is 500 RWF and each week adds 500 RWF more. It runs on a 5% base rate.

How much can I earn in the Interest jar?

4.0% a year. You can put money in and take it out any time. A 12-month deposit pays its interest upfront and locks the principal.

How does a Picker get paid?

Each order card shows the fee, distance and stops before you accept. Lunch orders between 12:00 and 14:00 add 300 RWF. Earnings settle every Wednesday, or you can cash out to MTN MoMo from 1,000 RWF.

Are the numbers on this site real?

They come from the itunda prototype app. Rates, prices, shops and names are samples and may change before launch.

When can I download the app?

The app is in prototype. Leave your email below and we will tell you when it opens.
