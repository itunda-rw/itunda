From components to real product moments.

Pattern completion contract

Patterns are not complete until they specify the journey around the components.

Entry — how the customer arrives and what context is preserved.
Happy path — the shortest successful journey.
Loading / empty / error — what the customer sees when reality is imperfect.
Recovery — retry, edit, cancel, support or safe exit.
Security — sensitive actions, verification and confirmation.
Accessibility + localization — keyboard, TalkBack/VoiceOver, long copy and Korean/Kinyarwanda/English content.
Platform parity — equivalent behavior across Web, Android and iOS without forcing identical layouts.

Product rule

IDS should make the right behavior easy to reuse. Product teams still own the customer problem, content and business logic.
