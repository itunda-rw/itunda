01 · Principles

Quiet by default.
Precise when it matters.

Identity is Itunda's. Public references inform craft and principles; implementation, semantics and assets remain original to Itunda.

Clarity

One dominant action, readable hierarchy and minimal decoration.

Native behavior

Compose, SwiftUI and Web can differ in implementation while preserving the same semantic contract.

Worst-case first

Loading, empty, error, disabled, long content and large text are part of completion.

Accessible by contract

Focus, screen readers, touch targets, reduced motion and localization-safe content are first-class.

04 · Accessibility

Accessibility is a component feature.

Large text reflows, controls expose purpose and state, touch targets remain comfortable, and reduced motion removes decorative movement without hiding functional feedback.

Large text

Content remains usable at increased system text sizes.

Screen readers

TalkBack and VoiceOver receive semantic role, state and label information.

Reduced motion

Functional state changes remain understandable without decorative animation.

Touch targets

Minimum 44px/pt with 48px recommended for primary interactions.
