02 · WHAT ITUNDA PROVIDES

One app.
Many financial jobs.

These are the product areas represented in Itunda's codebase — from real money movement and merchant payments to financial products and the services planned around them.

↗
Money movement · built

Send, request & pay.

Move RWF between people, pay merchants, generate QR payment intents and handle bills and airtime flows.

◎
Financial control · built

See your money clearly.

Wallet balances, transactions, contacts, spending views and savings goals put the user's financial state first.

▣
Banking & savings · built / expanding

Save with a plan.

Savings goals, deposits and interest flows are part of the backend product surface, with broader banking capabilities designed to expand.

%
Credit · built / expanding

Borrow with context.

Loan offers, applications and repayments are implemented, with a larger Rwanda lender marketplace planned around them.

⌁
Invest · built / expanding

Grow your portfolio.

Stock positions and trading flows exist in the product surface, with RSE-first investment expansion mapped in the product architecture.

+
Protect · built / expanding

Keep protection close.

Insurance enrollment and premium flows are implemented, with broader policy and claims experiences in the roadmap.

QR
Merchant · built

Businesses can get paid.

Merchant registration, QR-style payment intents, collection, fees and ledger-backed settlement are implemented in the backend.

★
Rewards · expanding

Benefits around activity.

Rewards, cashback, referrals and engagement are part of Itunda's product system and mini-app direction.

Built today ≠ every future capability.Itunda is explicit about the difference.
Source-grounded. Product status is based on implementation visible in the repository; expanding and roadmap items are intentionally labeled rather than presented as finished features.
